11.3 Reflection
← Back to Mathematics Hub

Reflection = Mirror Image

Object and image are on opposite sides of the axis of reflection at equal perpendicular distances.

Object → Axis = Axis → Image

x-axis

(x,y) → (x,-y)

UP ↔ DOWN

y-axis

(x,y) → (-x,y)

LEFT ↔ RIGHT

Quick Check

Reflect Q(-3,2) in the y-axis.
